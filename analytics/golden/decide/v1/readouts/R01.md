# Readout R01

> **Real users.**

Public Hillstrom e-mail test (MineThatData, 2008): customers who got the men's e-mail versus no e-mail. These are real shoppers, not TravelPlan users. Metric: dollars spent within two weeks.

## Sample ratio

| Arm | Users | Planned share | Observed share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| control | 21306 | 50.0% | 50.0% |
| treatment | 21307 | 50.0% | 50.0% |

Chi-square = 2.35e-05 (1 df), p = 9.96e-01.

## Primary metric: mean value per user

| Control | Treatment | Difference (T − C) | Relative lift | 95% CI | p |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|---:|
| 0.6528 | 1.423 | +0.7698 | +117.9% | [+0.4851, +1.055] | < 0.0001 |

## Plan

- Welch t-test, two-sided, alpha = 0.05; estimates are treatment − control.
- Planned horizon: all 42613 enrolled users (the primary table above).
- Sample ratio alert when the chi-square p < 0.0005.
